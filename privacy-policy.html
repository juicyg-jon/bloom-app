Bloom — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8/6/2026

Bloom is designed to work entirely on your device. This policy explains what that means in plain terms.

What we collect

Nothing. Bloom does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information, usage data, or analytics. There are no accounts, no logins, and no servers involved in how the app works.

Where your data lives

Your progress (points, garden stage, streak, achievements, settings, and any photos you add) is stored only in the app's local storage on your own device. It never leaves your device, and we — the developer — have no access to it at any time.

Photos

If you choose to add your own photos in Settings, those photos are stored locally on your device only, for use within the app's Picture Match and Puzzle Pieces games. They are never uploaded, transmitted, or shared anywhere.

Camera / Photo Library access

Bloom requests access to your photo library only when you choose to add a photo yourself. This permission is used solely to let you pick a photo — nothing is accessed automatically or in the background.

Third parties

Bloom does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services of any kind.

Children's privacy

Bloom does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy is ever updated, the new version will be posted at this same address.
