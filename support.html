Bloom — Support

Need help with Bloom? Here are answers to common questions, and how to reach out.

How do I add my own photos?

Open Settings (the gear icon on the home screen) and scroll to "My Photos." Tap "Add Photo" to choose pictures from your photo library. They'll then be available in Picture Match and Puzzle Pieces under the "My Photos" theme.

How do themes unlock?

Every completed game earns Bloom Points, shown as a growing garden on the home screen. New themes unlock automatically as your garden grows.

Will I lose my progress if I miss a day?

Your points, garden, and achievements are never lost. Only your daily streak resets if a day is missed — everything else stays exactly as it was.

Can I change the text size or turn off sounds?

Yes — open Settings for text size, sound effects, read-aloud instructions, and a reduced motion option.
